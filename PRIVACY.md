# Privacy Policy for ViLM

**Effective Date:** August 7, 2026

## Overview

ViLM (Video Library Manager) is built with your privacy as a fundamental priority. Your personal media collection is your business, and ours.

## Data Collection and Storage

**ViLM does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data with its developers.**

- All data generated by the app — video thumbnails, contact sheets, actor profiles, custom tags, playlists and scene markers — is stored entirely locally, alongside your video files or in whichever location you chose for your library.
- The `.catalog` database that powers your library sits directly beside your video files.
- There are no analytics, no tracking frameworks, and no crash reporters that phone home.
- Your video files themselves are never uploaded anywhere.

## Network Access

**ViLM works fully offline, but it is no longer offline-only.** The app supports optional metadata plugins that look your library up against a third-party service to fill in details you would otherwise type by hand.

**Nothing leaves your device unless you install a metadata plugin, supply your own credential for that service, and start a lookup.** With no plugin configured, the app makes no network requests at all beyond any web link you click yourself.

When you do run a lookup, here is exactly what is involved.

### What is sent

| Sent | When |
|---|---|
| A **perceptual fingerprint** of a video — a short digest computed on your device from a handful of frames | Matching a video |
| **Search text** you already hold: a video's title, its cast names, a studio name, an actor or studio name | Matching a video, actor or studio |
| Your **credential** for that service, as it requires | Every request |

⚠️ **Your video files are never transmitted.** The fingerprint is computed locally and is a digest, not footage — it cannot be turned back into your video.

### What comes back, and where it goes

Metadata (titles, dates, studios, cast, tags, biographical details) and images (actor photos, studio logos). All of it is written into your local library. Nothing is sent onward.

### Your credential

The credential you supply for a metadata service is stored in the **system Keychain**, per plugin — never in preferences, never in a plain file, and never in your library's catalog. It is not included in library backups or in the export/merge files used to move data between two copies of your library.

### Choosing not to use it

Metadata lookup is entirely optional. If you never install a plugin, ViLM behaves exactly as an offline application: it will scan your files, read your filenames, build your library and browse it with no network access whatsoever.

## Third-Party Services

Where you configure a metadata plugin, your use of that service is governed by **that service's own privacy policy and terms**, not by this one. ViLM does not control what a third-party service logs about requests made to it, and you should read its policy before supplying a credential.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
