# Privacy Policy for ViLM

**Effective Date:** June 28, 2026

## Overview
ViLM (Video Library Manager) is built with your privacy as a fundamental priority. We believe that your personal media collection is your business, and ours. 

## Data Collection and Storage
**ViLM does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data.** 
- All data generated by the app (including video thumbnails, contact sheets, actor profiles, and custom tags) is stored entirely locally on your device or your personal iCloud Drive, depending on where you choose to save your library.
- The `.catalog` database file that powers your library is generated and stored directly alongside your video files.
- We do not use analytics, tracking frameworks, or crash reporters that phone home.

## Network Access
ViLM does not require an internet connection to function. The only time ViLM interacts with external networks is if you explicitly click an external web link that you manually added to an Actor Profile or Video metadata.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
